# 🌱 Treense Mobile App

**Treense** is a mobile application that uses image classification to determine the health of **Falcata seedlings**. Designed for consumers buying seedlings, Treense provides a **quick, offline-capable**, and **easy-to-use** way to identify healthy and unhealthy plants in just seconds.

---

## 📱 Features

- 📸 **Capture or Upload Images** – Instantly scan a seedling using your phone camera or upload a photo from your gallery.
- 🧠 **Powered by MobileNetV2** – Uses a pre-trained deep learning model for accurate classification.
- 📊 **Instant Results** – Get results in seconds with health status and confidence level.
- 🌐 **Offline Capability** – Works offline for image analysis (with limited features).
- ☁️ **Optional Upload** – Upload scan results to the server when connected to the internet.
- 🕓 **History Log** – Keeps a record of all scans made by the user.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Android (APK)
- **Model:** MobileNetV2 (TensorFlow Lite)
- **Backend:** Supabase (for online sync, classification uploads)

## 🚀 How to Use

### Step 1: Launch the App  
- Tap **"Start Classifying!"**

### Step 2: Choose an Input  
- Tap **“Capture Image”** to take a new photo  
- Or tap **“Upload Image”** to select from your gallery

### Step 3: Get Instant Results  
- See if the seedling is **Healthy** or **Unhealthy**
- If online, view additional details like **Confidence %** and **Location**

### Step 4: Upload Results *(Optional)*  
- Tap the **Upload** button to save your scan to the cloud for record-keeping

## 🧠 About Treense

Treense solves three key problems:
- ❌ **Misclassification** due to lack of knowledge
- 🕒 **Time-consuming** health checks in large nurseries
- ⏱️ **Delayed disease detection** due to visual-only inspection

Using AI and mobile technology, Treense makes classification **faster**, **easier**, and **smarter** for everyone.
